# Acme Bank Internal AI Usage Policy (FICTIONAL)

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. Acme Bank does not exist. This policy was written for the CompliBot demo to
simulate a confidential internal document that only "internal" users may retrieve. It is not
legal advice and is not based on any real organisation's policy.

1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This policy sets the rules for using generative AI and machine learning tools at Acme Bank.
It applies to all employees, contractors and third-party service providers who use AI tools for
Acme Bank work.
1.2 This policy should be read together with the MAS Technology Risk Management Guidelines and the
Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

2 APPROVED TOOLS
2.1 Employees may only use AI tools listed on the Approved AI Tools Register maintained by the
Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Public consumer chatbots are not approved for Acme Bank
work.
2.2 A business unit that wants to add a new AI tool must submit an AI Risk Assessment to the
Model Risk Committee, which decides within 20 business days.

3 DATA HANDLING
3.1 Customer personal data, including NRIC numbers, phone numbers and email addresses, must not be
entered into any AI tool unless the tool is approved for Restricted data and the data has first
been redacted or pseudonymised.
3.2 Data classified as Confidential or Restricted must not leave Acme Bank's approved cloud
regions in Singapore.

4 HUMAN OVERSIGHT
4.1 Any AI-generated content sent to customers must be reviewed and approved by a human employee
before it is sent.
4.2 AI systems must not make final decisions on credit applications. A credit officer must review
every AI recommendation and record the reason for the final decision.

5 MODEL RISK MANAGEMENT
5.1 Every AI model in production must be recorded in the Model Inventory with its owner, purpose,
training data sources, and date of last validation.
5.2 High-risk models must be independently validated before deployment and re-validated at least
once every 12 months.

6 INCIDENTS
6.1 Employees must report any suspected AI-related incident, such as data leakage through a prompt
or harmful output sent to a customer, to the CISO within 24 hours of discovery.
6.2 The CISO decides whether the incident must be escalated to the regulator under the bank's
incident management procedures.
